# HEALTHCARE-REPORT-ANALYSIS

Project Overview:
This interactive Healthcare Dashboard was developed in Microsoft Excel to provide a comprehensive overview of hospital operations, patient demographics, billing performance, and healthcare trends. The dashboard enables healthcare administrators to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) and make informed decisions through interactive filters and visualizations.

Dashboard Objective:
The objective of this dashboard is to
Monitor patient statistics
Analyze billing performance
Track hospital utilization
Understand demographic trends
Identify common medical conditions
Evaluate insurance coverage
Compare hospital and doctor performance

Dashboard Insights
1. Patient Distribution by Gender
Female: 27,774
Male: 27,726
  The patient population is almost perfectly balanced between males and females, indicating no significant gender disparity in healthcare utilization.
2. Medical Conditions
The most common conditions include Arthritis. Diabetes, hypertension, obesity, cancer, asthma
  Arthritis and Diabetes appear to have the highest patient counts, suggesting these chronic diseases account for a significant portion of hospital visits.
3. Patient Distribution by Age
The largest patient segment is 71 years and above with approximately 12,142 patients
The smallest group is 11–20 years with approximately 2,443 patients
  Healthcare demand increases significantly among elderly patients, highlighting the need for stronger geriatric healthcare services.
4. Top Hospitals by Patient Volume
The dashboard compares the five hospitals with the highest patient counts.
  Patient volume is relatively evenly distributed across the leading hospitals, indicating balanced service utilization.
5. Blood Group Distribution
The dashboard analyzes patients across different blood groups.
  Most blood groups have similar patient counts, with O+ appearing slightly lower than others in this dataset.
6. Insurance Providers
Major Insurance providers include Cigna, Medicare, UnitedHealthcare, Blue Cross, Aetna
  Cigna covers the highest number of patients, making it the dominant insurance provider within this dataset.
7. Yearly Patient Trend
The dashboard tracks patient numbers from 2019–2024.
Observations
Growth from 2019 to 2020
Stable patient numbers through 2023
Sharp decline in 2024
   The significant reduction in 2024 may indicate incomplete data collection or a reporting period that has not yet concluded.
8. Admission Type
Admissions are categorized into Elective, Urgent, Emergency
   The distribution appears relatively balanced, indicating the hospital manages a mix of planned procedures and emergency care.
9. Top Doctors
Ranks physicians based on patient count.
Michael Smith treated the highest number of patients among the displayed doctors, indicating high patient engagement or specialization.

Key Business Insights
